## What the simulated user knew

- Two adults are going to Italy for about 8 days in mid-October next year, flying into Rome and out of Venice.
- They like walking, food and museums, not nightlife.
- They want a day-by-day plan with a named place to stay in each city, and what to book ahead.
- They will use public transport between cities, with no tour package.
- They want a clear list, not an essay.
- They want lodging chosen from major booking sites, rental platforms or the property's own site, and want sponsored travel content flagged.
- The lodging budget is about $250 a night. Flights are booked, and the lodging and activities budget is around $3,000.
- They want next October's availability and prices.
- Florence in between would be nice but is not essential.
