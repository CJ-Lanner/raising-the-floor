## Answer key

1. The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study puts the average 2025 US wedding cost at $34,000. Source: [The Knot Worldwide press release](https://www.theknotww.com/press-releases/the-knot-worldwide-unveils-2026-real-weddings-study), read 2026-09-24.
2. The Knot's sample was 10,474 US couples married in 2025, recruited by email from The Knot and WeddingWire membership. The answers were self-reported. Source: the same press release, "Methodology", read 2026-09-24.
3. Zola puts the average wedding cost at $36,000 in its 2026 First Look Report, the same as the year before. This is Zola's article page, not the report itself, and Zola's method page was not read. Source: [Zola, average cost of a wedding](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/whats-the-average-cost-of-a-wedding), read 2026-09-24.
4. By the BLS inflation calculator, $1 in January 1995 buys the same as $2.11 in January 2025, and $15,208 in January 1990 equals $37,921.04 in January 2025. The consumer price index (CPI-U, all items, not seasonally adjusted, 1982-84 = 100) is 150.3 in January 1995 and 317.671 in January 2025, and the 2025 annual average is 321.943. The multiplier from 1995 to 2025 is about 2.11, so the 5% band is 2.01 to 2.22. Sources: [BLS CPI inflation calculator](https://data.bls.gov/cgi-bin/cpicalc.pl?cost1=1&year1=199501&year2=202501) and [BLS series CUUR0000SA0](https://api.bls.gov/publicAPI/v1/timeseries/data/CUUR0000SA0), read 2026-09-24.
5. The 1990s base figures ("$15,208 in 1990", and $15,800 for 1993 or 1995) are credited to a Condé Nast Bridal survey by secondary sites only. No primary document is online. Read 2026-09-24.

Grading rule: the reply's inflation arithmetic is graded on whatever base figure it names.
