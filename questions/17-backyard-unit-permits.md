## Answer key

1. SDCI says a detached unit needs a construction addition/alteration permit. Changes to electrical service may also need Seattle City Light. Source: [SDCI, accessory dwelling unit (backyard cottage)](https://www.seattle.gov/sdci/permits/common-projects/accessory-dwelling-unit%C2%A0(backyard-cottage)), "What Permits Do You Need?", read 2026-09-24.
2. With the city's pre-approved plans for a detached ADU, the permit usually comes in 2 to 6 weeks. Source: the same SDCI page, read 2026-09-24.
3. Washington HB 1337 (2023), effective July 23, 2023, requires cities in urban growth areas to allow two ADUs per lot and to permit detached ADUs. Source: [HB 1337 session law, section 4(1)(c) and (d)](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/biennium/2023-24/Htm/Bills/Session%20Laws/House/1337.SL.htm), read 2026-09-24.
