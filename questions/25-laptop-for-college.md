# A laptop for college

Topic: shopping. Formats asked: quick question and detailed request. Task ids: D-B-02 (quick), D-F-02 (detailed).

## The question as asked

### Quick question

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> I need a new laptop for college, any recommendations?

The user who gives everything up front opened with:

> I need a new laptop for college, any recommendations? i'm a sophomore in college and need one laptop that will last me 3 or 4 years for papers, browsing, zoom and some excel. i carry it to class every day. hard cap is $800 before tax. needs 16 gb of ram and at least 8 hours of battery, and nothing heavy, 3.5 lb or under is my limit. no refurbished, no chromebooks, no gaming laptops. windows or mac both fine. buying from a major us store or the maker. give me one main pick and one backup with quick reasons, going off real testing like notebookcheck, rtings or wirecutter, and skip the sponsored lists.

## What the simulated user knew

- The user is a college sophomore who writes papers, browses, uses Zoom and some Excel, and carries the laptop to class every day.
- The current laptop is seven years old and dying.
- Windows or Mac are both fine.
- The user wants one laptop to last three to four years. A good answer is one pick with the reason, not a list of ten.
- The laptop must be new, from a major US retailer or the maker. No refurbished laptops, and no Chromebooks, because the user needs desktop Excel.
- The user wants a quick recommendation.
- The user wants reviews based on real testing (Notebookcheck, RTINGS, Wirecutter), and wants sponsored lists flagged.
- The budget is $800 before tax, a hard cap.
- The user wants a current-generation model.
- The laptop must weigh 3.5 lb or less, have 16 GB of RAM, and have a battery rated for a full class day (8 hours or more). No gaming laptops.
- The answer should be one pick plus one alternative.
