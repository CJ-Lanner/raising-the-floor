# When open enrollment starts

Topic: money. Formats asked: quick question. Task id: F-B-06.

## The question as asked

### Quick question

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> when does open enrollment start?

The user who gives everything up front opened with:

> when does open enrollment start? i'm self employed and buy my own insurance through pennie in pennsylvania. i need the dates for 2027 coverage.

The quick question does not say which open enrollment. Finding that out is the point of the question.

## What the simulated user knew

- The user is self-employed in Pennsylvania.
- The user buys health insurance through Pennie, the Pennsylvania state marketplace.
- The user wants the start and end dates of open enrollment for 2027 coverage.
- The user wants a one-line answer.
- The user wants the official site, pennie.com.

## The checklist

A good answer had to meet every check. A check that fits the person's situation tests whether the answer used the user's own facts. A check that gets the facts right tests whether the answer is right about the world.

| # | Check | Kind | Checked against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gives the dates for individual marketplace plans in Pennsylvania, whether or not it names Pennie, and not Medicare's or an employer's dates alone, after asking or being told. | Fits the person's situation | The conversation |
| 2 | Gives November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027 as Pennie's open enrollment for 2027 coverage. If the reply gives December 15, it says December 15 is the deadline for coverage from January 1, not the end of enrollment. | Gets the facts right | The answer key |

## Answer key

1. Pennie's 2027 open enrollment runs from November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027. Pennie's board voted for these dates on July 23, 2026, and pennie.com states the same dates. Sources: [Pennie board minutes, July 23, 2026, page 2](https://agency.pennie.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Pennie-Board_Special-Session-Minutes_20260723_FINAL_OGC.pdf), [Pennie board deck, August 6, 2026, page 38](https://agency.pennie.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Pennie-Board-of-Directors-August-Final-Meeting-Deck.pdf) and [pennie.com, What's New](https://pennie.com/whats-new/), read 2026-09-25.
2. On pennie.com, enrolling by December 15 gives coverage from January 1. Enrolling from December 16 to January 15 gives coverage from February 1. Source: [Pennie FAQ](https://pennie.com/learn/faq/), read 2026-09-24.
3. The Pennsylvania Insurance Department's 2027 rate release (September 18, 2026) says open enrollment "typically" runs November 1 to December 15 for coverage starting January 1, 2027. Its December 15 is the deadline for January 1 coverage, not the end of the window. Source: [Pennsylvania Insurance Department, 2027 rate release](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/insurance/newsroom/pennsylvania-releases-aca-2027-health-insurance-rates.html), read 2026-09-24.
4. A federal court struck the rule that shortened the window. On June 12, 2026, the federal district court in Maryland (City of Columbus v. Kennedy) struck parts of the 2025 Marketplace Integrity rule. CMS then said the open enrollment dates in 45 CFR 155.410(e)(4) stand: November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027 on HealthCare.gov. Pennie's board papers say the court found the shorter-window rule unlawful. The government's appeal was not confirmed on an official page. Sources: [CMS statement, July 27, 2026, page 1](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/ftr-requirement-py26-27-statement.pdf) and [Pennie board deck, July 23, 2026, page 23](https://agency.pennie.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Pennie-Board-of-Directors-July-23-Special-Session-deck.pdf), read 2026-09-25.
5. The October 15 to December 15 dates were real but were replaced. Pennie's board adopted them on February 19, 2026, then reversed them on July 23. A Pennie press release from June 9, 2026 still shows the old dates. Sources: [Pennie board minutes, February 19, 2026, page 2](https://agency.pennie.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Pennie-Board-of-Directors-February-Meeting-Minutes.pdf) and [Pennie press release, June 9, 2026](https://agency.pennie.com/one-in-five-pennie-enrollees-drop-health-coverage-due-to-expired-federal-tax-credits-2/), read 2026-09-25.

Grading rule: November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027 passes. December 15 is not required, but a reply that gives it must call it the deadline for January 1 coverage. A reply that gives December 15 as the end of enrollment fails. October 15 to December 15 fails. November 1 to December 31 fails, because that window belongs to the federal rule the court struck.
