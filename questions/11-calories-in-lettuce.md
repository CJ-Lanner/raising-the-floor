# Calories in a head of lettuce

Topic: health. Formats asked: quick question. Task id: F-B-01.

## The question as asked

### Quick question

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> how many calories are in a head of lettuce

The user who gives everything up front opened with:

> how many calories are in a head of lettuce a whole head of iceberg, raw, the regular round one from the supermarket. i just need one number to log.

- The user is logging food in MyFitnessPal.
- The lettuce is a whole, raw head of iceberg, the regular round one from the supermarket.
- The user wants one number to log, with the variety it assumes.
- A one-line answer is enough.
- The user has no source preference. USDA is fine.

## Answer key

1. USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy entry "Lettuce, iceberg (includes crisphead types), raw" (fdcId 169248): 14 kcal per 100 g. Source: [FoodData Central 169248](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/food-details/169248/nutrients), read through the USDA API, 2026-09-24.
2. USDA FoodData Central, Foundation entry "Lettuce, iceberg, raw" (fdcId 2346388, published 10/28/2022): 17.11 kcal per 100 g by Atwater general factors (14.46 by specific factors). Source: [FoodData Central 2346388](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/food-details/2346388/nutrients), read through the USDA API, 2026-09-24.
3. USDA head weights: small 324 g, medium (6-inch diameter) 539 g, large 755 g. Source: [FoodData Central 169248](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/food-details/169248/nutrients), portions, read 2026-09-24.
4. The range: 14 kcal per 100 g gives 45 (small), 75 (medium) and 106 (large). 17.11 gives 55, 92 and 129. The key range is 45 to 130 kcal.
