# Travel insurance for a trip to Italy

Topic: money. Formats asked: quick question. Task id: D-B-09.

## The question as asked

### Quick question

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> going to Italy next spring, do I need travel insurance?

The user who gives everything up front opened with:

> going to Italy next spring, do I need travel insurance? 10 day trip, two adults, 41 and 43, $6,800 prepaid and mostly nonrefundable, all on a chase sapphire preferred. we have a ppo through my employer, no preexisting conditions, no adventure sports. i want a quick current recommendation for a us single trip policy under $250 total, based on the card's guide to benefits and the insurer's actual policy documents. explain what the card already covers for cancellation and where the gaps are for overseas medical and evacuation, and i don't want to pay for coverage the card already gives me. skip the affiliate comparison sites. end with a two line gap summary.

## What the simulated user knew

- The trip is 10 days in Italy next spring, for two adults aged 41 and 43.
- $6,800 is prepaid and mostly non-refundable, all booked on a Chase Sapphire Preferred card.
- The travelers have a PPO health plan through a US employer.
- They have no pre-existing conditions and plan no adventure sports.
- The user wants to decide whether to buy a policy and what kind. A good answer says what the card already covers and what gap is left.
- Only US-sold single-trip policies, not annual plans.
- The user wants a quick but specific answer.
- The user wants the card's Guide to Benefits and the insurer's policy documents as sources, and wants affiliate comparison sites flagged.
- The budget is under $250 for both travelers.
- The user wants the card's current benefits.
- The answer should say whether anything they already have covers medical care and evacuation abroad, and should not recommend a policy that repeats the card's trip cancellation cover without saying so.
- The answer should be a recommendation plus a two-line gap summary.

## Answer key

1. Chase Sapphire Preferred trip cancellation or interruption insurance covers up to $10,000 per covered traveler and $20,000 per trip, and $40,000 per 12 months per account. The current Guide to Benefits (BGC11599) took effect on 06/14/26, and the limits are unchanged from the 10/01/24 guide. Sources: [Chase Guide to Benefits BGC11599, page 29](https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11599.pdf) and [Chase Sapphire Preferred card page](https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred), both read 2026-09-24.
2. The current guide adds Emergency Evacuation and Transportation, up to $100,000, for trips of 5 to 60 days more than 100 miles from home. The guide has no emergency medical treatment benefit. Its Travel and Emergency Assistance service gives referrals only, and the traveler pays for any services. A reply saying the card has no evacuation cover is wrong on the facts. Source: [Chase Guide to Benefits BGC11599, page 8](https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11599.pdf), read 2026-09-24.
