# A cash-back credit card

Topic: money. Formats asked: quick question. Task id: D-B-03.

## The question as asked

### Quick question

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> thinking about getting a rewards credit card, which one is good?

The user who gives everything up front opened with:

> thinking about getting a rewards credit card, which one is good? i'm 29, credit score around 740, one card with no rewards, and i pay it off every month. about $600 a month on groceries and $200 on gas. i don't travel and don't care about points. what's a good cash back card for me, one main pick and one backup, current us consumer cards only, no annual fee ever, no store or business cards, and nothing that needs a bank account with them to get the best rate. give me each card's grocery and gas rates and what i'd actually earn on my spending. go by the card's own terms for the rates and fees, i don't trust the review sites.

## What the simulated user knew

- The user is 29, with a credit score of about 740.
- The user has one card with no rewards and pays it in full every month.
- The user spends about $600 a month on groceries and $200 a month on gas.
- The user does not travel and is not interested in points.
- The user wants one cash-back card for everyday spending. A good answer names the card, the reason, and what it earns on this spending.
- Only US consumer cards with no annual fee, including after the first year. No store cards and no business cards.
- The user wants a quick answer.
- The user wants rates and fees from the issuer's own terms page, and wants it flagged that most card review sites are paid by the card issuers.
- The user wants current offer terms.
- The user wants to avoid cards that need a banking relationship to earn the top rate.
- The answer should be one pick and one alternative, with the earn rate on groceries and on gas.

## The checklist

A good answer had to meet every check. A check that fits the person's situation tests whether the answer used the user's own facts. A check that gets the facts right tests whether the answer is right about the world.

| # | Check | Kind | Checked against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The pick has no annual fee. | Fits the person's situation | A lookup of the card named |
| 2 | The answer states the earn rate on groceries and on gas. | Fits the person's situation | The conversation |
| 3 | The earn rates and annual fee the reply states for each card it recommends match the issuer's terms page. Where a recommended card caps a bonus rate below this user's yearly spending in that category, the reply states the cap. | Gets the facts right | A lookup per card named |

## Answer key

1. Amex Blue Cash Everyday earns 3% at US supermarkets and 3% at US gas stations, each on up to $6,000 a year (then 1%), with no annual fee. On this user's spending ($7,200 groceries and $2,400 gas a year), the grocery cap binds: $180 + $12 + $72 = $264 a year. Source: [American Express, Blue Cash Everyday](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card/blue-cash-everyday/), read 2026-09-24.
2. Chase Freedom Unlimited earns 1.5% on all purchases (3% on dining and drugstores, 5% on Chase Travel), with no grocery or gas bonus and a $0 annual fee. Source: [Chase, Freedom Unlimited](https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited), read 2026-09-24.
3. Wells Fargo Active Cash earns unlimited 2% cash rewards on net purchases, with a $0 annual fee. Source: [Wells Fargo, Active Cash](https://creditcards.wellsfargo.com/active-cash-credit-card/), read 2026-09-24.
4. Citi Custom Cash is not a candidate. Citi has taken no new applications for it since May 28, 2026, so a reply that recommends it fails on "currently offered". Source: [Citi, Custom Cash](https://www.citi.com/credit-cards/citi-custom-cash-credit-card), read 2026-09-24.
5. Citi Double Cash earns 2% on purchases (1% when you buy, 1% when you pay), with no caps and no annual fee. Citi's Custom Cash page sends applicants here. The cash back is earned as ThankYou Points. Source: [Citi, Double Cash](https://www.citi.com/credit-cards/citi-double-cash-credit-card), read 2026-09-24.

Card issuers change their line-ups without notice.
