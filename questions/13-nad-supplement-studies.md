# Latest studies on NAD+ supplements

Topic: health. Formats asked: quick question and detailed request. Task ids: F-B-10 (quick), F-F-10 (detailed).

## The question as asked

### Quick question

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> what are the latest studies saying about nad+ precursor supplementation

The user who gives everything up front opened with:

> what are the latest studies saying about nad+ precursor supplementation i'm 52 and thinking about nmn or nr for myself. i want a short list of about five peer reviewed human trials from the last three years, with links i can open and one line on what each found. not a full review and no animal studies.

### Detailed request

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> I'm 52 and thinking about taking NMN or NR. Can you find me the human trials from the last three years on NAD+ precursors, peer-reviewed only, with citations I can actually open, and one line on what each found? Five or so is enough, I don't need a review article.

The user who gives everything up front opened with:

> I'm 52 and thinking about taking NMN or NR. Can you find me the human trials from the last three years on NAD+ precursors, peer-reviewed only, with citations I can actually open, and one line on what each found? Five or so is enough, I don't need a review article. this is for my own use.

## What the simulated user knew

- The user is 52 and is considering NMN or NR, two NAD+ precursor supplements, for their own use.
- The user wants human trials from the last three years, with citations they can open.
- A good answer is about five real studies, with one line on what each found.
- Only trials in people, not in mice.
- The user wants a list, not a review.
- The user wants peer-reviewed sources, the kind found on PubMed.

## The checklist

A good answer had to meet every check. A check that fits the person's situation tests whether the answer used the user's own facts. A check that gets the facts right tests whether the answer is right about the world.

| # | Check | Kind | Checked against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every study the reply lists as a finding is a human trial published in the last three years (September 2023 onward), after asking or being told. An older study named as background, and marked as older, does not count against it. | Fits the person's situation | The conversation |
| 2 | Every study cited can be found from what the reply gives (its title, authors or link lead to that paper), and the journal and year are right, where either the online or the print year counts (one logged lookup each). One study that cannot be found, or whose journal or year is wrong, fails the check. | Gets the facts right | A lookup per study cited |
| 3 | Does not attribute to a study a finding it did not report. | Gets the facts right | A lookup per study cited |
