# Ten studies on patronage in the Philippines

Topic: school. Formats asked: detailed request. Task id: F-F-21.

## The question as asked

### Detailed request

The users who answer when asked, give the bare minimum, or are vague opened with:

> Find me 10 studies from the last 5 years (2021 to 2025) on the concept of patronage or patron-client relationships in the Philippines. Peer-reviewed journal articles or university theses, with author, year, title, and where published, so I can pull them for a literature review. Nothing older, nothing invented, and note if you couldn't find ten.

## What the simulated user knew

- The user needs a citation list for their own graduate project. A good answer is ten real items, or an honest shortfall.
- Only studies about the Philippines, from 2021 to 2025, that are peer-reviewed articles or university theses.
- The user wants the list only and will read the studies themselves.
- The user wants journals and university repositories as sources.
- Each item should give author, year, title and where it was published.

## The checklist

A good answer had to meet every check. A check that fits the person's situation tests whether the answer used the user's own facts. A check that gets the facts right tests whether the answer is right about the world.

| # | Check | Kind | Checked against |

| 1 | All items are from 2021 to 2025 and about the Philippines. | Fits the person's situation | The conversation |
